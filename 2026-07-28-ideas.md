# Resource metadata ideas

Fields considered for a future expansion of `sources.yml`:

## Provider

`provider` would identify the organization, publisher, or creator responsible for a resource, such as `ASTRO`, `ARRO`, `ROECSG`, or `ACRO`.

## Access

`access` could use a controlled vocabulary:

- `free`
- `paid`
- `subscription`
- `institutional`
- `mixed`
- `unknown`

## Source sections

`source_sections` would preserve the original headings under which a resource appeared. This is especially useful when duplicate entries are merged and their tags no longer fully express provenance.

## Alternate URLs

`alternate_urls` would hold secondary destinations such as a podcast feed, video channel, downloadable document, or archived version. The existing singular `url` would remain the primary destination.

## Status

`status` could use a small controlled vocabulary:

- `active`
- `archived`
- `unavailable`
- `unknown`

This could be updated whenever links are verified.

## Edition and publication year

`edition` and `publication_year` would make books, guidelines, and recurring publications easier to filter and update without encoding version information only in the title.

## Featured and priority

`featured` or `priority` could support curated placement and deterministic ordering in a website or resource directory.
